Reading notes
This doc is the template & page tier of atomic design. No atoms/molecules: no type stacks, no spacing decisions, no final components. Blocks are labeled by intent, not by visual treatment. A developer or designer picking up any one template should be able to scope the page from the blocks alone.
Photography is reserved for 2026 shoot (Caleb's renderings, TBD) — all imagery is a hatched placeholder. When real shots land, they slot in as full-bleed in-situ architectural and maker-process frames per the brand archetype.
Archetype discipline
Everyman + Outlaw — approachable, grounded, confident, a little unexpected.
Not Hero. Not Ruler. If a layout feels monumental or aloof, it's wrong for this brand.
DO feelpeer-to-peer · shop-floor · dry wit · self-aware
DON'T feelaspirational · heritage · corporate · clinical
One accentVolt #95FF00 · once per screen
Noemoji · gradients · purple

Template wireframes

One wireframe per page type, not per page. 14 templates cover all 34 URLs. Each template lists its blocks by intent (not visual treatment), calls out persona-specific adaptations, and marks auth state + geo-aware surfaces.
How to read a template: left column = meta (path, persona weight, auth, powers, adaptations). Right column = blocks in page order, top to bottom. Each block row has a number, an intent tag (hero/cta/content/gate/etc.), a plain-English description, and a persona-adaptation box when it reads differently for dealer vs. individual vs. rep vs. A&D.

Follow-ups · open questions

Things that need a call before next fidelity pass.
  1. Series tier criteria. What categorizes a series as Flagship vs. Value vs. Quick-Ship? Owner: Product + Marketing. Blocks: series card UI, browse filter, compare table. Proposed default: one Flagship per collection, everything else Value, QS is an overlay (not mutually exclusive).
  2. Unmapped series SKUs. REVEL, APP, ELEMENT, ARTISAN, SOLO, TRIG don't have numeric codes yet. How do they render in URL paths (/products/revel vs. /products/s-042) and in CAD filenames? Blocks: Series Detail template, Resource downloads. Proposed default: slug-first paths (/products/revel), numeric codes revealed in spec sheet metadata only.
  3. 2026 photography pipeline. Caleb's renderings aren't delivered yet. How do we stage Series Detail + Spaces + Collections without the hero images? Blocks: every image-led template. Proposed default: placeholder tiles with hatch pattern; commit to publish on photography delivery (Q2 2026).
  4. A&D specifier vs. Dealer overlap. Some A&D firms also act as dealers. Can one account hold both roles? Blocks: auth gate C, portal role model. Proposed default: one account, multiple roles, role-switcher in header.
  5. Self-serve quantity ceiling. Brief says "≤ 9 units direct, ≥10 routes to rep." Is 10 the right line? Does it vary by series (Quick-Ship vs custom)? Blocks: Individual Buyer flow, checkout. Proposed default: 9 units across all QS SKUs; non-QS always rep-routed.
  6. Rep card behavior in unmapped territories. What surfaces when IP + zip don't match a rep? Blocks: pattern 03. Proposed default: generic "contact sales" card, logged in sales-CRM as house lead.
  7. X Spec pricing visibility. v2 assumes live price in-configurator. Does logged-out / non-dealer see MSRP? dealer-tier? a placeholder? Blocks: X Spec template. Proposed default: logged-out sees MSRP, dealer sees tiered, both visible at all times (not gated).
  8. Collections as IA vs. marketing motif. Currently tagged "A&D primary." Do Collections belong at /products/collections or at a separate top-level /collections? Blocks: sitemap. Proposed default: nested under Products (current), surface in Spaces-first mega-menu as well.
  9. CSI 3-part spec generation. Is this a downloadable PDF per series, or a configured document per spec? Blocks: A&D flow, Resource template. Proposed default: per-series PDF in v1, configured doc in v2.
  10. Quote tracking SLA. Dealer-priced quote returns in how long? Affects confidence in the flow. Blocks: quote submit template, portal. Proposed default: 24 business hours stated, tracked in portal.
