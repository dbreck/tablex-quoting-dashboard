03

Why This Stack

Five reasons every pick was intentional — not inherited, not trendy.

Data Sovereignty

Every piece of TableX data lives in one Postgres database we control (Supabase). No vendor lock-in for the things that matter.

One Deployment

Marketing site, dashboard, CMS admin, and Spec Studio configurator all ship from a single Next.js app. No microservices, no second database.

Competitive Parity

Industry-leading animation and CMS stack. GSAP + Framer Motion + Lenis, Payload CMS, Cloudinary.

Cost Discipline

10 of 14 services are free or open source. Only Vercel ($20/mo) and Supabase ($25/mo) cost money — both are non-negotiable infrastructure.

Verified, Not Assumed

Every compatibility question checked against Payload 3.81.0 (April 1, 2026) before being locked in. Postgres adapter, Next.js 16, Supabase — all confirmed.

🛡️
Danny's Priority: Data Ownership

Self-hosted and open-source options are preferred wherever possible. Payload CMS (MIT), Supabase (open core), and Postgres (OSS) mean TableX owns its data stack outright — no SaaS ransom risk.
